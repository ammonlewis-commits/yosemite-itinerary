Day 1 · Fri May 22 West to Central Valley

Enter from the west entrance and work east — zero backtracking. ~3 miles of driving total. Lunch near Yosemite Village between stops 3 and 4. Friday will be busy — arrive at Bridalveil trailhead by 7:30 AM before lot fills.

1
Bridalveil Fall
8:00 AM · 45 min 0.5 mi

First stop as you enter the valley — no driving past it and doubling back. The 620-foot cascade is shrouded in mist at this hour and the paved trail is easy for any age. Magical introduction to the park.

2
El Capitan Meadow · Valley Floor Loop
9:15 AM · 60 min 1–2 mi

Short drive east to the meadow. Walk 1–2 miles of the Valley Floor Loop here for jaw-dropping views of El Capitan rising 3,000 feet straight up. Totally flat, stroller-friendly. Look for climbers on the wall.

3
Lower Yosemite Falls
11:00 AM · 60 min 1 mi

The showstopper. Hit it before midday crowds. The roar and mist at the base are genuinely thrilling for kids. Grab lunch at Yosemite Valley Lodge or the Village Store, a 5-minute walk away.

4
Cook's Meadow Loop
12:30 PM · 45 min 1 mi

Just a 3-minute walk from the Yosemite Falls trailhead — knock it out right after lunch. Easy flat loop with Half Dome and Yosemite Falls perfectly framed across the meadow. Done by ~2 PM, afternoon is yours.

Day 2 · Sat May 23 East Valley — Shuttle Day

Park once at Curry Village and ride the free Valley Shuttle all day. Saturday is the peak Memorial Day crowd day — shuttle use is essential. Aim to be parked by 7:00 AM. Both hikes done by noon.

5
Mirror Lake Trail
8:00 AM · 90 min 2 mi

Shuttle Stop 17. Go early for the best Half Dome reflections in still, glassy water — it's one of the most iconic views in the park and kids love seeing the mountain "upside down." Best in spring before the lake dries up.

6
Happy Isles · Vernal Fall Footbridge
10:15 AM · 75 min 1.6 mi

Shuttle Stop 16 — just a few stops from Mirror Lake. The paved path to the footbridge winds alongside the roaring Merced River through an ancient forest. Stop at the footbridge; the upper Mist Trail is too steep for young children.

7
Stoneman Meadow
12:00 PM · 20 min 0.5 mi

Steps from the Curry Village parking lot — no shuttle needed. A flat, open meadow stroll with one of the best unobstructed Half Dome views in the entire valley. Zero elevation gain, perfect for tired legs, and an ideal spot to spread out a picnic lunch before heading home.

Backup Options
If You Have Time or a Hike Doesn't Work Out

Both backups are in Yosemite Valley, require no extra driving, and are easy enough for young children. Swap one in if a primary stop is too crowded, a trailhead lot is full, or the kids have more energy than expected.

Backup · Day 1
Swinging Bridge & Merced River Walk
~45 min 0.75 mi

A flat, shaded walk along the Merced River to a footbridge with direct views of Yosemite Falls. The river is cold and clear — kids can wade in the shallows on warm days. Picnic tables and restrooms on site. Located between El Capitan Meadow and Lower Yosemite Falls, so it fits naturally into the Day 1 route with zero detour.

Best swap for: El Capitan Meadow if the Valley Floor Loop lot is full, or as an add-on if you finish Stop 3 early and want a lunch spot with a view.

Backup · Day 2
Ahwahnee Meadow Walk
~20 min 0.5 mi

A gentle meadow loop in front of the historic Ahwahnee Hotel with sweeping views of Half Dome and the valley walls. Shuttle Stop 3 — easy to reach on Day 2 before or after Mirror Lake. Almost no foot traffic compared to the main trailheads, and the open meadow is perfect for kids to run around.

Best swap for: Stoneman Meadow if crowds around Curry Village are overwhelming, or as an add-on between Mirror Lake and Happy Isles on Day 2.

Know Before You Go · May 22–23, 2026

CrowdsThis is Memorial Day weekend — among Yosemite's 3 busiest days of the year. Both days require arriving before 7:30 AM. Parking lots fill by 9 AM.
No Reservations NeededNo timed entry permits are required to drive into Yosemite in 2026. Just purchase your entry pass at recreation.gov in advance to skip gate lines.
Park PassNon-residents now need the America the Beautiful Digital Pass ($250, covers up to 4 people age 16+, valid 1 year). Buy at recreation.gov before arrival.
Waterfall PeakLate May is prime waterfall season — snowmelt hits maximum. Lower Yosemite Falls and Bridalveil will be thunderous. Mirror Lake reflections are best before the lake shrinks in summer.
ShuttleThe free Valley Shuttle runs 7 AM – 10 PM. Day 2 uses stops 16 (Happy Isles) and 17 (Mirror Lake). On a crowded Saturday, shuttles can be packed — ride early.
Tioga Pass ClosedHwy 120 East (Tioga Pass) is expected to still be closed May 22–23, reopening around Memorial Day. Doesn't affect any Valley hikes, but there's no high-country access these days.
